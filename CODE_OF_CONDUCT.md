# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing somebody's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat —
and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at the address in the git
history. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may warn, remove a
comment, close a discussion, or block an account, depending on severity and
repetition.
